Questions

You might be wondering…

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Therapy often focuses on healing the past and mental health. Coaching focuses on your present and future — goals, choices and growth. If something needs a therapist, I'll gently let you know.

How are sessions held?

Online by video call, so you can join from anywhere in a quiet, comfortable space. Sessions usually last 45–60 minutes.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your goal. Many people feel real progress within 4–6 sessions. We decide together — there's no long contract.

Is the discovery session really free?

Yes. It's a no-pressure conversation to see if coaching is right for you, with no obligation to continue.

Do you help with addictions or bad habits?

Yes. I offer habit and de-addiction coaching for things like smoking, alcohol, phone or gaming. Coaching works alongside medical care, not instead of it — if you are physically dependent on alcohol or drugs, please speak to a doctor first. Free helpline: Tele-MANAS 14416.

Will my information be kept private?

Completely. Everything you share is confidential and never shared with anyone.
