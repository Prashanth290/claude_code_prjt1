Privacy

Your privacy comes first.

What you share in coaching is deeply personal. This page explains, in plain words, how it is protected.

What I collect

  • The first name or nickname you give me — your real or full name is not needed.
  • A phone or WhatsApp number so I can call you.
  • Anything you choose to write in the booking form.

I never ask for your address, ID documents, or card or bank details.

Calls are never recorded

Sessions are one-to-one voice calls. They are never recorded, and I take them from a private room so nobody else can hear.

Notes

I keep short session notes to support your progress. They use a client code instead of your name and are stored with a password and two-step login.

How your information is used

Only to arrange and deliver your coaching. It is never sold, shared or used for marketing. The booking form is delivered to my email by a form service (Formspree).

Deleting your information

Notes and messages are deleted within 6 months after coaching ends. You can ask me to delete everything sooner, at any time.

Testimonials

I only share a testimonial with your written permission and your approval of the exact words. You choose how your name appears: first name, initials or anonymous.

The one exception

Everything you share stays confidential, except if I believe you or someone else is in serious danger. In that case I may encourage you to contact emergency help, or contact it myself.

Coaching is not treatment

Coaching is not a substitute for medical or psychological care. If you are struggling to cope, please call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (free, 24x7), or 112 in an emergency.
