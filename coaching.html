What is coaching?

How is coaching different?

A simple way to understand where coaching fits.

Talking to a friend

Kind, but limited

  • Gives opinions and advice
  • Often says what you want to hear
  • No clear plan or follow-up
Life coaching

Clarity + action

  • Focuses on your goals and future
  • Helps you find your own answers
  • Clear weekly plan you can follow
  • Support and accountability
Therapy

Healing the past

  • Works on deep emotional wounds
  • Treats mental health conditions
  • Led by a licensed professional
