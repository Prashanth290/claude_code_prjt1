What is life coaching?

A space to think clearly — and a partner to help you act.

Coaching is a focused conversation about you: where you are, where you want to be, and what's in the way. I listen closely, ask the questions that matter, and help you turn insight into small, real steps — at your pace.

Coaching looks forward. We focus on your goals and your next steps, not on the past.
You lead, I guide. No lectures or ready-made advice — the answers come from you.
Action, not just talk. Every session ends with something clear you can do this week.

FAQ

Questions you might have

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Therapy often focuses on healing the past and mental health conditions. Coaching focuses on your present and future — goals, decisions and growth. If something needs a therapist, I'll gently tell you.

How are sessions held?

Online by video call, so you can join from anywhere in a quiet, comfortable place. Sessions are usually 45–60 minutes.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your goal. Many people see real progress in 4–6 sessions. We'll decide together — there's no long contract.

Is the discovery session really free?

Yes. It's a no-pressure chat to see if coaching is right for you. There's no obligation to continue.

Will my information be kept private?

Completely. Everything you share is confidential and is never shared with anyone.
