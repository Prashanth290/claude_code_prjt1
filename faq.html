FAQ

Everything you might want to know before booking your free session.

Questions

You might be wondering…

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Therapy often focuses on healing the past and mental health. Coaching focuses on your present and future — goals, choices and growth. If something needs a therapist, I'll gently let you know.

How are sessions held?

One-to-one by voice call — phone or WhatsApp — so you can join from anywhere in a quiet, comfortable space. No video, no camera. Sessions last about 45 minutes.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your goal. Many people feel real progress within 4–6 sessions. We decide together — there's no long contract.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no fee to start, and I never ask for card or payment details. The first call is a no-pressure conversation to see if coaching is right for you.

Then how does payment work?

Only after you feel real benefit. At our review call we look back at the goals you set. If coaching has helped, you can pay whatever feels fair and affordable to you, whenever you're ready. Paying nothing is also okay.

Why do you work this way?

I have five years of lived experience on this journey, and I'm confident these conversations can change a life. I don't want money to stand in anyone's way. Your progress — and, if you choose, your story — is what matters most to me.

Do you help with addictions or bad habits?

Yes. I offer habit and de-addiction coaching for things like smoking, alcohol, phone or gaming. Coaching works alongside medical care, not instead of it — if you are physically dependent on alcohol or drugs, please speak to a doctor first. Free helpline: Tele-MANAS 14416.

Will my information be kept private?

Yes — privacy is my highest priority. Calls are never recorded, you can use a first name or nickname, and notes are kept minimal and deleted after coaching ends. The only exception is if I believe you or someone else is in serious danger. Read the privacy policy.

Still have a question?

Ask me anything in your free 30-minute call — no pressure, no obligation.
